# Areas for Exploration: Optimizing AI Reliability at Certa

Drawing from my personal projects and operational background, here are 3 areas I’m eager to explore to help improve agentic performance at Certa:

### 1. Scaling Automated Evaluation Loops
I’ve been experimenting with LLM-as-Judge frameworks to provide a structured feedback loop for agent outputs. I’d love to see how this could be applied to Certa’s vendor risk reports to help human reviewers focus on high-complexity edge cases.

### 2. Efficiency through Multi-Model Routing
In my *Prompt Forge* project, I explored routing tasks to different models based on complexity. I’m curious if a similar approach at Certa could help optimize latency and token costs for routine vs. reasoning-heavy compliance checks.

### 3. Visibility via Operational Dashboards
I’ve found that real-time monitoring of agent health and task queues (as I built in *Mission Control*) is essential for maintaining trust. I’d be interested in contributing to any internal tooling Certa uses to track and manage agent performance.
